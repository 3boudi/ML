The bias-variance trade-off

The one curve that explains why a better fit can be a worse model.

  1. 01 Left: the model is too simple, so it misses the pattern.
  2. 02 Right: it memorises the noise, so it fails on new data.
  3. 03 The middle is the work: enough flexibility, not more.
