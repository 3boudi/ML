K-means in three panels

One pass of the algorithm, drawn instead of described.

  1. 01 Pick k centres. Give every point to its nearest centre.
  2. 02 Move each centre to the mean of its points.
  3. 03 Repeat until nothing moves. The answer depends on k and the start.
