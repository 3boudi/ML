Privacy & Cookies Policy

Updated: October 2026

Clear, transparent, and respectful of your autonomy. No trackers, no advertising, and no personal data collection.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

ML VII is an educational study guide for university students. We do not require accounts, logins, email addresses, or names. We do not track your IP address, browser fingerprint, or geographic location. Nothing you read or study here is ever sent to a tracking database.

2. How Local Storage (LocalStorage) is Used

Instead of using tracking cookies on a server, this website stores a few essential pieces of data directly inside your own web browser storage (LocalStorage). This data never leaves your device:

  • ml7-lang: Remembers your preferred interface language (Arabic or English).
  • ml7-caption-lang: Remembers your preferred YouTube caption language (Arabic, English, or Off).
  • ml7-watched: Stores an array of video IDs you have marked as watched to calculate your overall learning percentage (% of ML learned).
  • ml7-consent-v3: Remembers that you acknowledged the privacy & local storage banner.

3. Third-Party Services (YouTube)

Videos embedded in this site are hosted on YouTube (StatQuest channel). When you click to play a video, YouTube may set its own cookies and track playback metrics according to Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not control Google/YouTube cookies.
