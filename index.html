Chapter 3

Place, assign, move, repeat.

  1. Place k centres

    Start with three centres, chosen by hand or by k-means++.

  2. Assign each point

    Each point joins the nearest centre; the lines are not drawn, the distance is.

  3. Move to the mean

    Each centre moves to the mean of its points. Repeat until nothing moves.

The loop stops when the centres stop moving. That is the whole algorithm.
